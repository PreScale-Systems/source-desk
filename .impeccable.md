## Design Context

### Users
Sales, purchasing, accounts and dispatch staff at an MSME selling laptops. They currently track leads in Excel, source through WhatsApp/email and invoice in Tally.

### Brand Personality
Bold, clear, dependable. The creator chose a bold, colorful workspace; preserve operational readability.

### Aesthetic Direction
Confirmed creator preference: bold, colorful workspace. Use deep ink-blue navigation, saturated orange actions, warm neutral canvas, teal/blue/gold functional accents, dense readable tables, strong action hierarchy. SourceDesk is the provisional demo brand. No external brand assets are needed.

### Design Principles
- Keep customer requirements and source evidence visible across the workflow.
- Make stock, offers, quote approval and dispatch state explicit.
- Show exceptions and missing information in plain language.
- Distinguish sample data and rules from live integrations.
- Support keyboard use, reduced motion and small screens.
